# BUG-001: Cart accepts a quantity of 0 or less, allowing an order for Rs. 0

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Reported by / Date** | Lara / 2026-10-11 |
| **Environment** | macOS Tahoe 26.5.2, Google Chrome 155.0.8059.12 (arm64), https://automationexercise.com |
| **Module** | Cart |
| **Related** | TC-CART-13a, TC-CART-13b, FR-CART-10, OBS-12 |
| **Severity** | High — Orders can be placed with a quantity of 0 for Rs. 0, and negative quantities create negative totals, breaking order and payment logic. No money was lost because the negative payment failed. |
| **Priority** | High — The bug is in the main purchase flow, is easy to trigger by typing a number, and the fix (reject quantities below 1) is simple. |
| **Reproducibility** | Always (5 of 5 attempts with quantities 0 and -3) |
| **Status** | New |

## Preconditions
1. The user is logged in with ACC-01.
2. The cart is empty.
3. The Home page is open in Chrome.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. On the Home page, click **View Product** under Men Tshirt.
2. Clear the **Quantity** field and enter 0.
3. Click **Add to cart**.
4. Click **Cart** in the header (close the pop-up first if one appears).

## Expected Result
- After step 3: the "Added!" pop-up does not appear.
- After step 4: "Cart is empty! Click here to buy products." is displayed.

## Actual Result
- After step 3: the "Added!" pop-up appeared.
- After step 4: the cart showed Men Tshirt with Quantity 0 and Total Rs. 0.

## Additional Information
- **Order placed for Rs. 0:** continuing to checkout with quantity 0, the Total Amount was Rs. 0. After paying with fake card details, the "Order Placed!" page appeared, and the downloaded invoice showed "Hi Lara QA, Your total purchase amount is 0. Thank you".
- **Negative totals:** with quantity -3, the cart showed Men Tshirt with Quantity -3 and Total Rs. -1200, and the checkout showed a Total Amount of Rs. -1200. Paying led to an error page instead of an order (see the debug page bug, OBS-13).
- **Requirement:** the quantity rule (minimum 1) comes from simulated product owner answers (RQ-03). However, orders with zero or negative quantities are invalid on any shop, so this is a defect regardless of the simulated rule.
